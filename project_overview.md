# Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the global data professionals job market, with the core objective of developing a comprehensive dashboard that consolidates key insights about demographics, salary trends, educational backgrounds, tool preferences, and job satisfaction metrics.  

The dashboard is designed to help HR teams and leadership understand workforce composition and inform strategic decisions on recruitment, compensation, and talent retention.

The dashboard helps monitor the following business goals:

- Identify the most common profiles among data professionals (e.g., education level, tools used, country)
- Benchmark salary expectations by role and education level
- Evaluate perceived work–life balance and salary satisfaction to support retention strategies

**Key tracked metrics:**

- Number of survey participants  
- Average age of respondents  
- Average salary by job title and by education level  
- Most common programming language used  
- Work–life balance score  
- Salary happiness score  
- Country distribution of participants  
- Perceived difficulty of entering data roles

The analysis covers various roles, education levels, and countries. It offers a snapshot view of the current state of data professionals worldwide and serves as a foundation for future workforce planning.

---

# Business Context and Strategy

The main business need addressed by this dashboard is to provide leadership and HR teams with data-driven insights on the data professionals market landscape.  

By understanding the most common skills, demographics, and satisfaction levels, the company can:

- Improve hiring strategies and identify talent gaps
- Adjust compensation and benefits packages to align with market expectations
- Develop initiatives to improve employee satisfaction and retention rates

The dashboard also supports proactive planning for expansion into new markets by highlighting geographic distributions and identifying regions with high talent availability.

---

# Operational Use and Monitoring

The unified dashboard serves as a single source of truth for leadership and HR teams. It eliminates the need for fragmented reports or manual data consolidation, making it easier to access updated insights ahead of planning and review sessions.  

The dashboard is primarily used during quarterly strategy meetings or annual workforce planning sessions. In these meetings, executives and HR managers review key workforce trends, analyze demographic distributions, and discuss necessary adjustments to hiring and retention policies.

The dashboard enables quick identification of outlier roles or regions that may require additional focus or targeted initiatives. For example, if salary satisfaction is consistently low in a specific segment, it may trigger further analysis and adjustments to compensation strategies.

---

# Price Sensitivity and Product Performance

While this dashboard does not focus on product sales, it provides valuable context around perceived career entry difficulty and salary satisfaction, which indirectly inform employer branding and competitiveness in compensation offers.  

The insights about tool usage and education level distribution help assess the alignment of the current talent pool with strategic technical needs and inform future learning and development initiatives.
